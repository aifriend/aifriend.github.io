Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account or an internet connection?

No. MindForge has no accounts and no login, and the game plays fully offline. Nothing is sent anywhere. Only buying or restoring the full game needs a connection to the App Store.

Why are some game modes locked?

Digit Span is free. The other six modes are part of the full game, a single one-time purchase with no subscription. Tap any locked mode to see what it includes and its price in your country. XP and levels measure your progress; they no longer unlock modes.

I bought the full game. How do I get it on another device, or after reinstalling?

Sign in to the App Store with the same Apple Account and open Settings → Restore Purchases in MindForge. Members of your Family Sharing group get the full game too.

How do I ask for a refund?

Apple handles refunds for App Store purchases. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com. If a refund is granted, the six extra modes lock again; your progress is kept.

Where is my progress stored? Can I reset it?

Progress (XP, level, high scores, difficulty, settings) is stored only on your device. Use Reset All Progress in Settings to start over, or delete the app to remove all data. Resetting progress does not remove the full-game purchase.

How do I change the language or theme?

MindForge follows your device language by default; in Settings → Language you can force English or Spanish. Settings → Theme switches between dark and white.

Is my data collected?

No data is collected, ever. See the Privacy Policy.
