Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account or an internet connection?

No. MindForge has no accounts, no login, and works fully offline. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Why are some game modes locked?

Modes unlock progressively as you earn XP and level up. Keep playing the unlocked modes — each completed session awards XP toward the next unlock.

Where is my progress stored? Can I reset it?

Progress (XP, level, high scores, difficulty, settings) is stored only on your device. Use Reset All Progress in Settings to start over, or delete the app to remove all data.

How do I change the language or theme?

MindForge follows your device language by default; in Settings → Language you can force English or Spanish. Settings → Theme switches between dark and white.

Is my data collected?

No data is collected, ever. See the Privacy Policy.
