Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account or an internet connection?

No. MindForge has no accounts and no sign-in of its own, and every exercise plays offline. A connection is used only by Apple's services: by the App Store, to show the full game's price and to buy or restore it and, from version 2.0, to send a rating if you choose to leave one; and, from version 2.0, by iCloud sync and by the optional Game Center leaderboards.

What is free, and what does the full game unlock?

Digit Span is free for everyone. From version 2.0, the Memory Techniques lessons and their practices are free too, and every other exercise can be played once for free, so you can try it before you buy. The full game is a single purchase, paid once, with no subscription and no ads. It unlocks Spatial Recall, Dual Task, N-Back, Go / No-Go, Rule Switch and Chunking Challenge, and from version 2.0 Quick Glance as well, for good. Tap a locked exercise to open the unlock screen, which shows the price in your country; from version 2.0, a locked exercise opens it once you have played its free session. Unlock the Full Game in Settings opens it at any time. Experience points (XP) and levels measure your progress; they never unlock exercises.

From version 2.0, a free session is one complete session of that exercise. Leaving it before the results, or closing the app during it, still uses it up. The short practice run that N-Back, Rule Switch and Quick Glance offer does not use the free session; the real session you start from the practice results does.

I bought the full game. How do I get it on another device, or after reinstalling?

Sign in to the App Store with the same Apple Account, open MindForge's Settings and tap Restore Purchases. Members of your Family Sharing group get the full game too, without buying it again.

How do I ask for a refund?

Apple handles refunds for App Store purchases. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com. If Apple grants the refund, the exercises the full game unlocked lock again, and your progress is kept.

Where is my progress stored?

On your device. From version 2.0, a copy also goes to your own iCloud account while Sync Progress with iCloud is on in Settings, which it is by default. That copy brings your progress back after you reinstall the app, and it appears on your other iPhone or iPad signed in to the same Apple Account. It holds your XP, sessions played, the difficulty and best score of each exercise, and your 400 most recent session records. Your settings, the purchase and the names of your memory palace stops are not part of it; restore the purchase with Restore Purchases.

Deleting the app removes everything it stored on the device. Before version 2.0, that deletes your progress for good. From version 2.0, a copy saved while sync was on stays in iCloud, even if you turned sync off later. After you install the app again, sync is on, and the app reads that copy back. Neither copy reaches the developer.

My progress does not show up on my other device. What can I check?

This applies from version 2.0. Check that both devices are signed in to iCloud with the same Apple Account and that Sync Progress with iCloud is on in MindForge's Settings on both. Open MindForge on the device that has the progress, then on the other one; iCloud can take a few minutes to deliver a change, and both devices need a connection.

When both devices have progress, MindForge combines them: it keeps the larger XP total and the larger number of sessions played, the better best score of each exercise, and the session records from both. XP earned on two devices at the same time is therefore not added together; the larger total is kept.

How do I reset my progress?

In Settings, tap Reset All Progress and confirm. The reset deletes your XP, level, best scores, difficulty and sessions played and, from version 2.0, your session history. It keeps your settings and the full-game purchase and, from version 2.0, the free sessions you have already used and the names of your memory palace stops. From version 2.0, scores already sent to Game Center stay on the leaderboards.

From version 2.0, when iCloud sync is on, the reset also empties the iCloud copy, and each of your other devices with sync on deletes its progress from before the reset the next time it reads iCloud; sessions played on it after the reset stay. A device that was offline does this once it receives the reset; if it sent its older progress to iCloud first, the reset reaches it the next time MindForge is open on one of your devices that has already applied the reset. A device whose sync is off when another device is reset keeps its progress until you turn sync back on there; it then deletes its progress from before the reset and keeps the sessions played after it.

From version 2.0, when iCloud sync is off, the reset clears only this device, and iCloud and your other devices keep their progress. If you turn sync back on later, the progress saved in iCloud comes back to this device, unless one of your other devices was reset with sync on in the meantime. To start over everywhere, reset while sync is on. The confirmation message says which of the two will happen before you confirm, and the Privacy Policy describes this in full.

Why did an exercise get harder or easier?

Before version 2.0, most exercises go up one step after a session in which at least 80 percent of the rounds were right, and never go down on their own; N-Back already moved up or down after each block of trials. From version 2.0, each exercise sets its difficulty from your answers, up or down, so you train close to your limit. Digit Span, Spatial Recall, Dual Task, Chunking Challenge and Quick Glance go up one step after three right answers in a row and down one step after a mistake. N-Back and Go / No-Go adjust after each block of trials, and Rule Switch adjusts once, at the end of the session. You can also change an exercise's difficulty yourself with the minus and plus buttons on its instructions screen.

What are Today's Workout and the weekly goal?

From version 2.0, the main menu offers Today's Workout, a short set of exercises chosen for the day. With the full game it holds three exercises of different kinds, taking in each kind the one you played longest ago; without it, it holds Digit Span forward and backward. An exercise is ticked off when you finish a session of it that you started from the workout card.

The weekly goal is three training days a week. A day counts when you finish at least one session on it, Memory Techniques practices included; practice runs do not count. The chart button at the top of the main menu opens Progress, with your weekly goal and a chart for each exercise.

How do the Game Center leaderboards work?

From version 2.0, the leaderboards are off until you turn on Game Center Leaderboards in Settings and sign in to Game Center. Then the score of each session you finish goes to that exercise's leaderboard, where other Game Center players, such as your friends, can see it next to your Game Center nickname. Show Leaderboards in Settings opens them. Practice runs, scores of zero and Memory Techniques practices are not sent. Turn the switch off to stop sending scores.

How do I add the widget?

From version 2.0, MindForge has a widget that shows how many days you have trained this week against your weekly goal, and today's workout progress; the medium size also shows your level. It updates after each session.

On the Home Screen, touch and hold an empty area until the apps jiggle, tap Edit and then Add Widget (on iOS 17, tap the add button in the top-left corner instead), search for MindForge, and choose the small or the medium size. On the Lock Screen, touch and hold it, tap Customize, choose the Lock Screen, tap the area under the clock and add MindForge. A Lock Screen widget can be seen without unlocking the device.

How do I change the language or the theme?

MindForge follows your device's language. In Settings, Language can instead set English or Spanish and, from version 2.0, Portuguese (Brazil), French, German, Italian or Japanese. In Settings, Theme switches between Dark and White.

I hear no sounds. What can I check?

MindForge plays its sounds as system sounds, so they follow the device's ringer volume and stay silent while the device is in Silent Mode. Check the Ring/Silent switch or the Action button on iPhone, or Silent Mode in Control Center on iPad, then the ringtone and alert volume in the device's Settings app, under Sounds & Haptics on iPhone or Sounds on iPad, and finally the Sound Effects switch in MindForge's Settings.

Is my data collected?

No. MindForge collects no data, and the app sends the developer nothing. The Privacy Policy describes what stays on your device and what goes to Apple's services.
