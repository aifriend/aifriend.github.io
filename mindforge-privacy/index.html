MindForge Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

TL;DR

MindForge collects no data. The game plays entirely offline. Your game progress stays on your device and is never sent anywhere. The one optional purchase is handled by Apple's App Store, not by MindForge.

Data Collection

MindForge does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The game plays entirely offline, and the app's own code makes zero network requests.

Purchases

Digit Span is free. The other six modes are unlocked by one optional in-app purchase, which Apple processes through the App Store using your Apple Account. MindForge never sees your payment details, your name, or your Apple Account. It only receives Apple's confirmation that the purchase was made, which stays on your device so the modes stay unlocked. Buying the purchase or restoring it needs a connection to the App Store; playing does not. Apple's handling of purchases is covered by Apple's own privacy policy.

Local Storage

All game progress (XP, levels, high scores, difficulty, settings) is stored locally on your device in a JSON file within the app's sandboxed directory. This data is never sent to any server, third-party service, or cloud backend.

Third-Party Services

MindForge does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, tracking, crash reporting, AI services, or cloud services. No third-party SDKs are integrated.

Tracking

MindForge does not track users across apps or websites. No advertising identifiers, device fingerprints, or behavioral data are collected.

Children's Privacy

MindForge is rated 4+ and is suitable for all ages. Because no data is collected from any user, no special considerations apply under COPPA, GDPR, or other children's privacy regulations.

Your Rights

Because no personal data is collected, there is no data to access, correct, delete, or port. Game progress is stored only on your device and is fully under your control. Use the in-app Reset button or delete the app to remove all data.
